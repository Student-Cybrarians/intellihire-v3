# Product Requirements Document: IntelliHire v3

## 1. Executive Summary
IntelliHire v3 is an AI-powered career intelligence and placement platform designed to transition users from passive job seeking to active, data-driven career management. It provides a persistent, AI-unified view of a user's career, specialized modules for assessment and interview preparation, and aggregate readiness reporting.

## 2. Product Modules
1. **Career Intelligence & ATS**
2. **Adaptive Online Assessment**
3. **Technical Interview Simulator**
4. **HR/Behavioral Interview**
5. **Hiring Committee & Readiness Aggregator**

## 3. Technology & Infrastructure
- Next.js 15, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, shadcn/ui.
- Cloudflare: Pages (hosting), D1 (database), R2 (file storage), KV (sessions), Workers AI.
- Auth: NextAuth.js v5 (Google OAuth + credentials).
